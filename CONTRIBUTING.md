# Contributing to APO

APO is open so that it can be tested and improved by people who do not work at SGAI Studio. Fork it, open an issue, or send a pull request.

## What helps most

1. **Evidence.** Data for or against a law: request logs, citation tracking, before-and-after measurements. Negative results count.
2. **The open proposals** in section 7 of the standard. Each lists what evidence it still needs.
3. **Checks as code.** Scripts that run a law's check against a site.
4. **Field reports.** What happened when you applied a law, including when it did nothing.
5. **Translations and adaptations** for a sector or a country.

## The rules for changing a law

1. **Amend, never silently rewrite.** Every change is listed in the change log with its date and reason.
2. **Law 3 applies to APO itself.** A change to a law comes with a number, a method, a date and a primary source, or with a test anyone can run. "I believe" is a reason to open an issue, not to change a law.
3. **One change per pull request.**
4. **Nothing that deceives an agent.** Prompt injection, cloaking, fabricated reviews and similar techniques are breaches of Laws 8 and 9 and will not be merged.
5. **No client data without consent.** Anonymise, or do not include it.

## Who decides

SGAI Studio Pte. Ltd. stewards versions 1.x and decides what is ratified into them. Ratified versions are published with their SHA-256 digest and logged publicly. If you disagree with a decision, fork: the licence exists for that.

## Forks

A fork keeps the credit ("based on APO, started by SGAI Studio Pte. Ltd., UEN 202620727K") and says what it changed. That is all the licence asks. Tell us and we will link your fork from the README.
