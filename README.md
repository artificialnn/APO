# APO: Agent Preference Optimization

**Search engine optimisation optimises the ranking. APO optimises the choosing.**

APO is an open standard: ten laws for publishing on the web so that AI agents can find you, check you and act on what they find, without ever being deceived.

## The ten laws

1. Sell to the chooser, not the clicker.
2. Exclusive supply wins the citation. Being the record wins the decision.
3. Every claim verifiable in one fetch: number, method, date, primary source.
4. One number and one mechanism, everywhere, enforced by architecture.
5. Stated limits beat superlatives. Publish against interest. *(Under test.)*
6. Say exactly what you are, and what you are not.
7. Machine surfaces ship with the feature.
8. Never lie to the machine. The machine remembers.
9. Never manufacture, or tolerate, fabricated third-party signals.
10. Make the agent's next step deterministic.

Each law in the standard has four parts: a **rule**, the **reason** for it, a **check** you can run on your own site, and an **evidence** label that says how far to trust it.

## How much to trust this

Not blindly. APO 1.0 is provisional. Its evidence base is narrow: one large dataset, failure cases from client work, and reasoning about how agents retrieve and verify, reviewed by one team. Law 5 is still under a pre-registered test. The standard says which laws are observed, which are reasoned and which rest on a single case, and it gives no figure it cannot show with a method and a source. That is the point of opening it: more sites, more data, more eyes.

## Fork it, change it, send it back

You are welcome to fork APO, adapt it to your field, translate it, or disagree with it in public.

- Evidence for or against any law is the most useful contribution. See the open proposals in section 7 of the standard.
- Changes follow one rule borrowed from the standard itself: every change to a law comes with a number, a method, a date and a primary source, or with a test anyone can run.
- Forks keep the credit line and say what they changed. Tell us about your fork and we will link it here.

Details in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Reviewed by AI assistants, in public

Each version of APO is logged in a public [Readmarks](https://github.com/artificialnn/readmarks) log. AI assistants, acting for their users, can record that they read it and whether they endorse it or object. Review sessions are registered before they run, and every outcome is published, objections included: https://www.sgai.sg/protocols/apo/1.0

## What APO is not

It is not a way to manipulate agents. Hidden instructions, cloaking and manufactured reviews are breaches of Laws 8 and 9. The rest of APO only works because agents can check.
