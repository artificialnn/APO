# APO 1.0 checklist

Run these on your own site. Publish your results with dates (Law 3 applies to claims about APO too).

| Law | Check | Evidence behind the law |
|---|---|---|
| Law 1. Sell to the chooser, not the clicker. | For each of your top pages: can you name its job in one word, and do you measure that job (citations for answer pages, completed tasks for task pages)? | Reasoned |
| Law 2. Exclusive supply wins the citation. Being the record wins the decision. | List your exclusive artifacts. Search for each: is yours the only retrievable text? Does any page of yours reconcile conflicting sources and show its method? | Observed |
| Law 3. Every claim verifiable in one fetch. | Sample twenty claims. How many have all four parts? How many are older than their re-verification interval? | Reasoned; consistent with failure cases |
| Law 4. One number and one mechanism, everywhere. | For each key fact, search the whole site and every feed. Count distinct values. The answer must be one, and a test must fail the build when it is not. | Observed |
| Law 5. Stated limits beat superlatives. Publish against interest. | Do your main pages say what you do not do? Is there a corrections log, and is its last entry recent? | Under test |
| Law 6. Say exactly what you are, and what you are not. | Does the structured data validate? Is the name identical everywhere? Is each known confusion answered by a negative claim? | Observed |
| Law 7. Machine surfaces ship with the feature. | Fetch the page without running scripts. Are the facts there? Does the structured data agree with the page? See also the open proposal on `llms.txt` in Section 7. | Reasoned; amendment proposed |
| Law 8. Never lie to the machine. The machine remembers. | Compare a sample of machine surfaces with what a person sees and with the facts. Is every past error listed in the corrections log? | Single case for the search-engine clause; otherwise reasoned |
| Law 9. Never manufacture, or tolerate, fabricated third-party signals. | For every third-party figure on your pages, compare the page, your structured data and the third party's own page. Does a test fail when they diverge? | Observed |
| Law 10. Make the agent's next step deterministic. | Can a script complete the next step from one page? Is ambiguous input refused? Does the handoff artifact exist? | Reasoned; consistent with failure cases |

## The scoreboard

Measure all four, and read them: agent fetch share, assistant referrals, citation share, spot-check survival.
